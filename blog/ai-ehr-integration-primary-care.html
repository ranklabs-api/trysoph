Sophia AI was built specifically for primary care, and that means we built it to work with the tools practices already use — not to replace them.

The Integration Question Every Practice Asks

The first question almost every practice owner asks is: "Will this work with our EHR?" It makes sense. Practices have invested years building their workflows around specific platforms like Athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, Tebra, Elation, and DrChrono. No one wants to disrupt what's already working.

The answer is yes. Sophia connects directly to the major EHR platforms independent practices use, and it does it in a way that requires no workflow changes for your staff. When Sophia answers a call and books an appointment, it goes straight into your calendar — the same calendar your front desk sees. No duplicate entry, no manual transfer, no "the patient showed up and we had no record of them" moments.

More Than Scheduling: Insurance Verification at the Source

One of the biggest sources of claim denials in primary care is front-end eligibility errors — wrong insurance information, expired coverage, missing prior authorizations. These errors don't get caught until billing runs the claim weeks later, and by then the revenue is already at risk.

Sophia verifies insurance eligibility on every call, in real time, before the appointment is booked. If coverage is active, the appointment goes on the calendar. If there's an issue — lapsed coverage, wrong payer, missing authorization — Sophia flags it immediately so your staff can address it before the patient ever sets foot in the office.

This one integration point alone can prevent thousands of dollars in denied claims per month. For the average independent primary care practice, that's real money that would otherwise slip through the cracks.

Sophia is a cloud-based AI that connects through standard, HIPAA-compliant APIs. Setup is measured in days, not months. Your existing EHR, scheduling, and billing systems stay exactly as they are — Sophia just adds a layer of automation on top of them.
